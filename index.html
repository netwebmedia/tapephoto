Photography for businesses in La Serena and Coquimbo

I’m Carlos Martinez, a photographer based in Coquimbo, Chile. The portfolio above comes from motorsport, concerts, aerial drone work, street and travel photography. The same eye now works for local businesses across La Serena, Coquimbo and the whole Coquimbo Region that need real photos of their team, premises and property instead of stock images.

There are four services: aerial and drone photography, a real estate photo plus drone package, motorsport and event coverage, and business and brand sessions. Every quote is custom and arrives via WhatsApp, with starting prices published on the services page (also available en español). Drone flights are planned according to DGAC regulations, and usage licensing is agreed per project.

Three dedicated pages, written in Spanish for the local market, explain how the work goes for each type of client:

Fotografía inmobiliaria →

Real estate: interiors, exteriors and drone aerials in one visit, delivered ready for property portals. For brokers and real estate firms.

Hoteles, cabañas y turismo →

Hotels, cabins and tourism: rooms, common areas and aerials for Booking, Airbnb and your own website.

Fotografía para empresas →

Businesses: team portraits, your premises and your products, so one session gives you months of content.

The blog has practical Spanish-language guides on preparing a shoot, photo counts, delivery formats and usage rights — start at the blog index.

I believe photography is about capturing the energy that usually moves too fast for the naked eye.

Frequently Asked Questions

What photography services do you offer in La Serena and Coquimbo?

Four services: aerial and drone photography, a real estate photo plus drone package (interiors, exteriors and aerials in one visit), motorsport and event coverage, and business and brand sessions with team portraits, workspace and product shots. There are dedicated Spanish-language pages for real estate, for hotels, cabins and tourism, and for businesses.

How much does a photo session cost?

Published starting prices are CLP $100,000 for aerial and drone photography, CLP $150,000 for the real estate photo and drone package, CLP $250,000 for motorsport and event coverage, and CLP $150,000 for business and brand sessions. The final quote is sent via WhatsApp once the location and scope are known.

Which areas do you cover?

Carlos is based in Coquimbo and works across La Serena, Coquimbo and the whole Coquimbo Region in Chile. Sessions elsewhere in Chile are possible, with travel quoted separately.

Are drone flights allowed at my location?

Drone flights are planned according to DGAC (Chilean civil aviation) rules and the conditions of each site. Locations near airports, over crowds or in protected areas may need extra permits and lead time, so mention the location when you get in touch.

How do I book a session?

Message Carlos on WhatsApp at +1 (442) 385-4585, use the contact form on the Contact page, or email carlos@netwebmedia.com. Tell him the type of session, the location and the date, and he replies with a quote.
